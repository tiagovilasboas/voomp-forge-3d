# UX/3D review — Voomp Forge 3D (shots: scratchpad/shots/qa)

## 1. Top 5 visual issues
1. **Overview is a dark, low-contrast island map** (qa-01, qa-02). Rooms float on near-black void; huge empty margins left/right/below; the scene fills ~55% of the canvas and HUD panels cover the west/south. No ground plane, horizon or atmosphere to anchor it. Hub cyan glass walls are far brighter than the orange sectors, so the hub dominates and sectors read as secondary.
2. **Sector floors are empty** (qa-13, qa-15): 4-5 desks and 2-3 racks occupy one band; the rest of each room is flat dark. Rooms feel unfinished vs. the dense hub.
3. **Inconsistent holographic language** (qa-11, qa-13, qa-16): hub uses cyan wireframe glass + rings; sectors use orange trim + opaque black racks (monoliths with tiny text, qa-13 left racks); gate is a solid mint slab; WeekBoard is an opaque white whiteboard (qa-15) that clashes with the holo style. Racks read as black boxes, not holograms.
4. **Floor signage/labels fight the cables** (qa-13, qa-15, qa-16): room titles ("ADMIN", "SUSTENTACAO N3") overlap flow dots, the sector holo tag and desk monitors; subtitle text runs off the room edge and is cropped ("adm…", "Plataforma, ferramentas"). Letters are huge, subtitle is tiny mono, low contrast.
5. **Corridor/plaza reads empty and ambiguous** (qa-01, qa-16): long dark road with dashes, staging dock and gate far apart; flow dots (yellow/orange/green) are the only motion and have no legend on-scene. Selected rack (qa-20) is clear, but unselected racks show no at-a-glance status except a small red "!" orb.
